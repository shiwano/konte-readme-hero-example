# Critique with no subagent

The pass you run yourself when the human sends you on without a subagent — it stands in for `konte-direction-critic`'s critique of the direction, narrower.

## Out of reach

- **You can't unread your own intent** — the critic's instrument is walking the cut cold and saying, shot by shot, what it takes to have happened. You already know what every beat means, so you cannot produce that read.
- **Every judgment riding on it goes with it** — whether a beat reads, an action is unambiguous, a payoff lands, a sequence rises. Their evidence is a first viewer's impression, and yours is not one.
- **Don't hand the gap to the human** — "please check whether shot 03 reads" is your missing pass wearing a question mark.

## What still holds — artifact against artifact

An axis survives when **both sides are on the page**, and the check is whether they match:

- **Deletion** — cut the shot: does anything downstream stop working?
- **Merge** — are trigger and reaction separate shots that one action would carry?
- **Setup / payoff** — every plant collected, every collection planted.
- **Causality** — position, possession, movement and state connect across a continuous-space stretch; the world's ledger never resets.
- **Roster match** — every `characters` / `props` entry an `action` names by its exact `name`, every `setup` an id the roster declares, and every setup's `location` one the location roster declares.
- **Brief compliance** — an element `brief.outOfScope` bans is a finding wherever it shows; whatever `brief.tolerances` accepts is closed on every axis.
- **Risk concentration** — does the payoff rest on one demanding shot with nothing to fall back on? What a given model can hold is its guide's question (`production-guide`).

## How to look

- **Re-open `direction.ts` and read the shots in order, as written** — never from memory; memory is where your intent lives.
- **Report a shot only when it is formally valid and still weak as a cut** — `framing`, `duration`, lens `role` and the pacing lines are the direction check's.
- **What the human already accepted is settled** — `konte inspect direction`. It is the fixed reference the rest must meet, so where settled and unsettled work clash the finding lands on the unsettled side.
